Walk the stack from the top. The interface is ordinary React. The domain band is the product: planner, SM-2 scheduler, weakness detector and gamification, all local logic. The services band can fail without stopping study - the AI service falls back across six models and repairs malformed JSON; the file parser handles PDF, DOCX and OCR. The data band is Dexie over IndexedDB with 40 tables. Firebase Auth is optional identity, drawn dashed because it needs the network. The point to land: no server is required for the study loop.
--- 7
This is the processing logic in one pass. Study actions are written locally; the weakness detector reads the last seven days and applies fixed weights: a failed coding attempt adds 30, low accuracy adds half of the shortfall below 100, a pomodoro adds 10, a successful coding attempt subtracts 20 and generating notes subtracts 10. The result is clamped to 0–100 and flagged at 40. Flashcards use SM-2, so the interval grows with the ease factor. Say that the weights are simple and inspectable on purpose — no black box.
